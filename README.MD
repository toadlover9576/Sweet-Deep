Hello and welcome to my fast-paced productivity suite, Sweet Deep Enhanced! 
To keep things simple, I'll give a brief overview of what it is, what it does, features, why I made it, etc.

What It Is:
-Productivity Suite 
-Multiple modes and features to get you back on track
-Relaxes and refocuses overstimulation
-Helps users get things done in a timely manner when they think of it

Features:
-Quick-Hit task organizer with degrees (Urgent, Critical, Standard)
-4 Breathing modes for McCollough research-based focus restoration (box, 4‑7‑8, resonant, energizing) 
-Focus Timer: Set it for how long you need to lock in on something you want to do
-Focus Meter: Measures pixel count, time variables, cursor movement, and feature usage to determine your focus
-Session Log: Shows when you began using Sweet Deep and completed tasks and focus timer so you can see quick snippets of progress

Why I made It:
-I loved making Bilon and use it practically ever day but needed something for when I wanted to do quick tasks (like feed my chickens!) on the go 
-It was actually supposed to be what Tranquil Anchor is now and I didn't want it to go to waste since I imported most of the original assets
-The name "Sweet Deep" was too cool to give up so I had to make a neon purple program with that "deeply sweet" vibe!
-Satisfaction 100% was a major driver for enhancing this program

Some background info:
-Began working on this (og version) back in 2023 on my Xbox Series X
-Didn't really do much with it until prob latter half of 2024 
-Used to have localstorage but it began to break with most of the widgets and menus on a blank background; lost alot of stuff switching to IndexDB and DexieJS
-Really started to work on Tranquil Anchor in early 2025 as a neat side project for a lot of pscyhology and research reports from books (Psychology of Persuasion, Charisma Myth, How to Win Friends & Influence People, etc.)
-Imported most of the assets from this and transitioned to redesigning with box and card elements
-Compressed messy JS and CSS logic into index.html for Sweet Deep Enhanced so I didn't have to jump around between .lib files and .style all the time (online html packager for libraries used)
-It was REALLY FUN to use Google's hex color feature to decide on super cool neon layouts

I hope anybody trying it out loves it!
